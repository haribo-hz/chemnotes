#### de broglie
de Broglie:
$$
\begin{equation}
\lambda=\frac{h}{{ mv }}
\end{equation}
$$
- note $p=mv$ (momentum)
- made generalisation to all matter
- everything has a wavelength, it's just negligible for "big" things
	- however, not negligible for tiny things like electrons

de Broglie wavelength signifies the point at which a particle's wave-like properties become significant
- only important for subatomic particles

1 angstrom
$1Å=1\times10^{-10}\pu{ m}$
