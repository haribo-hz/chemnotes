radial distribution function:
- describes the probability of an electron being at a particular distance from the nucleus
- plot of probability density against $r$

![[radial distribution function for 1s, 2s, 2p.png]]

s orbitals have layers, so multiple peaks are observed in the RDF

peaks at higher values of $r$ mean that if the electron is further away, it is higher energy

values of $r$ where the probability $=0$ are called radial nodes
the number of radial nodes can be found by $n-l-1$

![[radial distribution function of 3d vs 4s orbital.png]]

4s is lower in energy than 3d on average despite its peak being further away from the nucleus because of the local maxima close to the nucleus
- this is why it is filled first
