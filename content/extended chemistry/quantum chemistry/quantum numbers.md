quantum numbers:
- principle quantum number ($n$)
$n=1,2,3,\dots,k,k\in \mathbb{N}\setminus{0}$
gives information about the main energy level

- orbital angular momentum quantum number ($l$)
$l=0,1,2,\dots,n-1$
gives information about the subshell the electron inhabits

- magnetic quantum number ($m_{l}$)
$m_{l}=-l,-l+1,\dots,0,\dots,l$ 
gives information about which orbital in the subshell the electron is in

- spin projection number ($m_{s}$)
- see [[quantum spin]]
$m_{s}=+\frac{1}{2}\text{ or }-\frac{1}{2}$
electrons have to have spin as they are fermions (which also have to have half-integer spins)
the consequence of spin is that electrons have magnetic moments and thus charge
