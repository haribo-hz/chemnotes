symmetry operation → 3D transformation leaving molecule
"apparently unchanged"

symmetry element → axis/point/plane with respect to which the operation is carried out


types:
#### $E$
element: whole of the molecule
operation: identity

#### $C_{n}$
element: n-fold axis of rotation
operation: rotation by 360˚/n

a molecule can have multiple rotation axes
the principle axis is that with the highest value of n

#### $\sigma$
element: mirror plane
operation: reflection

$\sigma_{v}$ vertical → contains principal axis
$\sigma_{h}$ horizontal → $\perp$ to principal axis

#### $i$
element: centre of inversion
operation: inversion (passing each point through the center of inversion and out to the same distance on the other side of the molecule)

#### $S_{n}$
element: improper rotation axis
operation: rotation by 360˚/n and reflection in plane perpendicular to rotation axis
