formation:
3˚/4˚ can be created by:
- SN2 halogenoalkanes using $\ce{NH_{3}(\text{ethanol})}$
- good because primary amines and secondary amines are better nucleophiles than ammonia, therefore multiple substitutions can take place
- thus not ideal for 1˚

formation:
1˚ can be created by:
1. SN2 halogenoalkanes using $\ce{CN^{-}(\text{ethanol})}$, heating under reflux to form nitrile
2. reduce to $\ce{RNH_{2}}$ using $\ce{LiAlH_{4}(\text{ether})}$ or $\ce{H_{2}}$ with $\ce{Pt/Ni/Pd}$ catalyst
