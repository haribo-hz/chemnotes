#further #chem
Fehling's test tests for aldehyde
- blue to red
- from $\ce{Cu^{2}+}$ to $\ce{Cu^{+}}$

Tollens' reagent ammoniacal silver nitrate $\ce{[Ag(NH_{3})2]+(aq)}$ tests for aldehyde
- prepared from silver nitrate, sodium hydroxide, ammonia solution
- positive observation: silver mirror formed

2-4,dinitrophenylhydrazine (brady's reagent 2,4-DNPH) tests for carbonyls
- positive observation: produces orange crystal
- lone pair on $\ce{-NH_{2}}$ (part of hydrazine) attacks electron-deficient carbon of carbonyl

$\ce{SOCl2}$ and $\ce{PCl_{5}}$ tests for presence of $\ce{-OH}$ (carboxylic acids and 1˚ and 2˚ alcohols)
- lone pair on oxygen attacks electron-deficient phosphorus(V) atom
	- because $\ce{O=P}$ bond very strong
- turns $\ce{-OH}$ into good LG
- SN2 substitution reaction after attached to phosphorus
- observation: $\ce{HCl(g)}$ evolved, test with conc. $\ce{NH_{3}}$ for thick white fumes, and phosphorus oxychloride or $\ce{SO_{2}(g)}$

Alkaline aqueous iodine $\ce{I_{2}(aq)}$ iodoform reaction tests for methyl ketones and secondary alcohols
- positive observation: produces pale yellow precipitate

bubble gas through limewater $\ce{Ca(OH)2(aq)}$ tests for $\ce{CO_{2}(g)}$
- $\ce{CO_{2}(g) +Ca(OH)2(aq)->H_{2}O(l) +CaCO_{3}(s)}$
- positive observation: cloudy
