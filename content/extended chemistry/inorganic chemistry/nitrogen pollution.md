$\ce{NO}\text{ and }\ce{NO_{2}}$
- produced naturally in lightning
- man-made in internal combustion engines

forms acid rain
![[nitrogen dioxide to nitric acid.png|672]]

catalyses oxidation of atmospheric sulfur dioxide
$$
\ce{NO_{2} +SO_{2}\overset{}\longrightarrow{} SO_{3} +NO}
$$$$
\ce{NO +\frac{1}{2}O_{2}\overset{}\longrightarrow{} NO_{2}}
$$

forms peroxyacetyl nitrates when reacting with unburned hydrocarbons

![[peroxyacetyl nitrate.png]]
