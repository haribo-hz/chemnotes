reactions of sodium bromide with sulfuric acid:
$$
\ce{NaBr +H_{2}SO_{4}\overset{}\longrightarrow{} NaHSO_{4} +HBr}
$$
$$
\ce{2HBr +H_{2}SO_{4}\overset{}\longrightarrow{} Br_{2} +SO_{2} +2H_{2}O}
$$

reactions of sodium iodide with sulfuric acid:
$$
\ce{NaI +H_{2}SO_{4}\overset{}\longrightarrow{} NaHSO_{4} +HI}
$$
$$
\ce{2HI +H_{2}SO_{4}\overset{}\longrightarrow{} I_{2} +SO_{2} +2H_{2}O}
$$
$$
\ce{6HI +H_{2}SO_{4}\overset{}\longrightarrow{} 3I_{2} +S +4H_{2}O}
$$
$$
\ce{8HI +H_{2}SO_{4}\overset{}\longrightarrow{} 4I_{2} +H_{2}S +4H_{2}O}
$$
