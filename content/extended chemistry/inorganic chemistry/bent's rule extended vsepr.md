Bent's rule: more electronegative atoms prefer orbitals with less $s$-character
- $s$-character orbitals more electronegative tend to draw electrons into the nucleus
- whereas $p$-character orbitals are more electropositive, weaker, and further away from the nucleus

for example in sp3d trigonal bypiramidal:
- the axial positions have 50% $p$ character (formed by $p_{z}$ and $d_{z^{2}}$)
- the equatorial positions are formed by mixing $s$, $p_{x}$, and $p_{y}$ orbitals 33%

so in $\ce{PCl3F2}$, fluorine would take axial positions
