on phase boundaries, $\Delta G=0$ (equilibrium exists)

$$
dG=vdp-sdt
$$

clausius clapeyron can be used…

considering two phases $\alpha$ and $\beta$ and two points on their phase boundary:
$$
\begin{align} 
dG_{1}&=dG_{2}=0\\
v_{\alpha}dp-s_{\alpha}dt&=v_{\beta}dp-s_{\beta}dt \\
\implies \frac{dp}{dt}&=\frac{s_{\alpha}-s_{\beta}}{v_{\alpha}-v_{\beta}}
\end{align}
$$
